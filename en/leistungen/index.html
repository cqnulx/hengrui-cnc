How we work

From drawing to finished part

  1. Clarify requirements

    Confirm the application, drawing revision, material, tolerances, quantities, and delivery requirements.

  2. Plan the process

    Review manufacturability and agree on critical dimensions, inspection points, and delivery arrangements.

  3. Machine and deliver

    Arrange production and in-process inspection, then deliver parts and records as agreed.
